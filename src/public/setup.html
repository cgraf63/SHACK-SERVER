SHACK-SERVER SETUP

The essentials in four short steps - everything else can wait.

1 · STATION
2 · TRANSCEIVER
3 · QRZ.COM
4 · DONE

Your station

Your callsign is used for the cluster login and the log, the locator for distances, beam headings and "heard near you".

Transceiver

Connect the transceiver with USB and switch it on. SHACK-SERVER then reads frequency and mode, and spots are tuned with one click.

Hamlib transceivers (Kenwood, Elecraft, Xiegu, Flex …) need the model list and are set up in Settings → Transceivers. You can finish this assistant first.

QRZ.com lookup

With a QRZ.com login SHACK-SERVER shows name, QTH and locator of the stations you work and fills them into the log. A free account works; the XML subscription gives data for every callsign.

Uploading QSOs to QRZ.com, Club Log or LoTW is set up separately in Settings → Log Uploads (link on the next page).

Ready to go

The basic setup is done. The dashboard shows spots, your transceiver and the band conditions.
