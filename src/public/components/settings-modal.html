Log Uploads

New QSOs are uploaded automatically in the background (with retry). Contest QSOs: QSO History → UPLOAD SESSION. LoTW: export your callsign certificate in TQSL (Callsign Certificates → right click → Save the Callsign Certificate File, .p12) and add it below - SHACK-SERVER signs the QSOs itself, TQSL is not needed on the Pi.

WSJT-X

QSOs logged in WSJT-X or JTDX are added to the log automatically (and uploaded like all QSOs). In WSJT-X: Settings → Reporting → UDP Server = IP of this server, port as below, "Accept UDP requests" on.

Split & CW

DXpeditions usually listen up: when a spot says "UP 2", "UP 1-3", "QSX 14025" or "LSN 14.205", TUNE sets the TX frequency on VFO B and switches split on. Tuning a spot without split switches split off again. Works with transceivers that support split control (Yaesu FTDX10, Yaesu FTX-1, Icom IC-7300, RGO ONE, Hamlib).

Used whenever a CW spot is tuned or CW is chosen without a sideband - on every band, not depending on the frequency.

Rotator

Antenna rotator via Hamlib (Yaesu GS-232, SPID Rot2Prog, Hy-Gain DCU-1, ERC, K3NG and many more). Turn the antenna from the live spots (click on the azimuth) or from the spot details.

Watch list notifications

The server checks every new spot against the watch list - also when no browser is open - and sends a push notification to your phone via ntfy. Install the ntfy app on the phone and subscribe to the same topic. The topic works like a password: choose a name nobody can guess.
