# Contributing to SHACK-SERVER

Thank you for your interest in SHACK-SERVER! Bug reports, feature ideas, radio-specific findings and pull requests are all welcome.

## Reporting bugs

Please open an issue and include:

- your transceiver model (e.g. FTDX10, FTDX101, FT-710, FT-991A) and firmware version
- how SHACK-SERVER is installed (Raspberry Pi, Linux PC, Docker, ...)
- what you did, what you expected and what happened
- relevant log output, with **all passwords, API keys and e-mail addresses removed**

## Pull requests

1. Open an issue first for larger changes, so we can agree on the approach before you invest time.
2. Keep pull requests focused on one topic.
3. Never commit personal settings or credentials. `data/settings.json` and `.env` are excluded from the repository for this reason.
4. Test your change with real hardware where possible and mention which radio you tested with.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Before your first pull request can be merged, you need to sign the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). A bot will ask you automatically when you open a pull request. Signing takes one click and is only needed once.

### Why a CLA?

SHACK-SERVER is published under the AGPL-3.0 and is also offered under a commercial license. Revenue from commercial licenses and services helps fund the ongoing development of the free version.

To make this possible, the project needs the right to distribute all code, including contributions, under both licenses. With the CLA you keep the copyright to your contribution; you only grant the project the rights it needs.

Your contribution will always remain available under the AGPL-3.0 as part of the free version.

## Questions

For questions about contributing, open an issue. For licensing questions, contact license@shack-server.com.

73!
